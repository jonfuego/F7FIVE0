- **Phone app: picks up where you stopped after the car is switched off.** Press Play in the car, on a headset or on the lock screen hours later and your podcast continues from the same spot, or your music continues with the same album or playlist, track and position. You no longer need to open the app first.
- A podcast episode you already finished no longer starts over from the beginning when you press Play.
- The car's Stop button now pauses, so Play carries on from there, and a podcast's place is saved when you stop.
- TV show pages now show a poster for each season.
- The Home page banner shows the album's description beside the title, up to a few whole sentences instead of a cut-off line.
- Admin > Updates now shows what changed under "What's new", in plain words, for every version you would get by updating, newest first.
- Security fix: your Sonarr and Radarr API keys are no longer written to the server log when an image download fails.
